# Coming from puzzle-boy v0.2.0

[puzzle-boy](https://github.com/hazeliscoding/puzzle-boy) v0.2.0 played 23 bonus stages after the original 20, with C to switch between Jack-o'-Lanterns and PageUp and PageDown to skip stages. Since the split into three repos, puzzle-boy plays only the original 20, as the original does. Everything else moved here.

## Play the bonus stages here

1. Download Community Edition for your system and unpack it ([install and troubleshooting](install-and-troubleshooting.md)).
2. Settings → Classic Nocturne → Import from My Steam Install. If your copy isn't in a Steam library, choose Import from a Folder or Zip and type the folder that holds `smt3hd.exe`, the same folder you gave puzzle-boy.
3. Stage select now has Classic: Original 20 and Classic: Bonus, the same 23 stages in the same order. With Theme on Auto they play in the original's look and sound.

## What's different

- **Switch:** C, or X on a pad, on any stage with more than one character.
- **No stage skip.** Stages unlock in order, with one skip: you can always play the two stages after each one you've cleared.
- **Undo and restart** are Z and R, and the pause menu has them too. Restart can be undone.
- **No Manikin Kid and no original dialogue.** Menus, the HUD and help are Community Edition's own.
- **Progress starts fresh.** puzzle-boy saves nothing, so there's nothing to carry over. Here, progress is saved per stage and kept if you remove or re-import the classic pack.

## Without a copy of Nocturne

The classic stages need your own copy. Without one, Community Edition still plays its own campaign, your stages and imported packs with its Default theme.
