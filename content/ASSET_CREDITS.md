# Asset credits

Everything in this folder ships with Puzzle Boy Community Edition. `provenance.json` lists the same files for tools, with each file's source, license and, for third-party files, its SHA-256.

## Original content

By the Puzzle Boy Community authors (see the git history), licensed Apache-2.0 like the code:

- the stages in `stages/`;
- the default theme in `themes/default/`: the Pixel Jack mark and the brand palette, from puzzle-boy's `docs/brand/`. The block colors are the Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe set;
- `manifest.json`, `provenance.json` and this file.

Pixel Jack is a pumpkin in a witch's hat, drawn from scratch for this project. It is not Nocturne's Jack-o'-Lantern.

## Fonts

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text ships beside them in `fonts/`.

- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (body text), version 1.006. Copyright 2020 Braille Institute of America, Inc. Designed by Elliott Scott, Megan Eiswerth, Linus Boman and Theodore Petrosky. From [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/atkinsonhyperlegible).
- **Silkscreen** (titles), version 1.001. Copyright 2001 The Silkscreen Project Authors. Designed by Jason Kottke. From [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen).
